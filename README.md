# ALUMINEX

### The AI-powered operating system for the global aluminium trading industry.

---

## 1. Vision
To be the definitive global operating system for physical non-ferrous metal trading—powering frictionless, automated, and intelligent aluminium transactions from recyclers and brokers to industrial end-users.

## 2. Core Features
*   **Structured Counterparty Databases:** Detailed profiles for aluminium buyers and suppliers including metallurgical preferences and logistics constraints.
*   **AI-Powered Lead Intelligence:** Global trade data parsing and customs log analysis to identify new trading opportunities.
*   **Automated Document Generation:** Instant, error-free generation of Sales/Purchase contracts, Packing Lists, and Commercial Invoices with Incoterms 2020 compliance.
*   **Aluminium CRM & Deal Flow:** Specialized pipeline management for physical metal trades with real-time margin tracking.
*   **Market Intelligence & LME Integration:** Live tracking of London Metal Exchange prices, physical premiums, and scrap-to-primary spreads.
*   **Email & WhatsApp Automation:** AI-driven quote drafting and shipment notifications for high-volume trade desks.
*   **Company Intelligence & Due Diligence:** Automated KYC, credit risk profiling, and fraud detection for metal counterparties.

## 3. Technology Stack
*   **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
*   **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, Pandas.
*   **Database:** PostgreSQL (via Supabase).
*   **Authentication:** Supabase Auth.
*   **AI/ML:** Specialized LLM implementations for document parsing and trade intent extraction.
