# ALUMINEX: Product Roadmap

ALUMINEX is an AI-powered operating system designed specifically for aluminium traders, importers, exporters, recyclers, brokers, and procurement teams. This roadmap outlines the strategic evolution of the platform into a comprehensive trading command center.

---

## Phase 1 — Sales OS
**Goal:** Fix the discovery and relationship bottleneck by digitizing the counterparty network and deal pipeline.

*   **Modules:** Buyer Database, Supplier Database, CRM, Lead Intelligence.
*   **Features:**
    *   Structured buyer/supplier profiles with metallurgical spec preferences.
    *   Aluminium-specific deal pipeline (RFQ to Settlement).
    *   Automated customs data parsing to identify trade leads.
    *   Real-time margin tracking per trade.

---

## Phase 2 — Trading OS
**Goal:** Automate the physical execution of trades and the heavy documentation burden.

*   **Modules:** Stock Management, Document Generator, Email & WhatsApp Automation.
*   **Features:**
    *   Multi-warehouse stock tracking with Certificate of Analysis (CoA) vault.
    *   One-click generation of Sales/Purchase contracts, Packing Lists, and Invoices.
    *   Incoterms 2020 compliance engine.
    *   Automated quote drafting and shipment status alerts via WhatsApp/Email.

---

## Phase 3 — Aluminium Intelligence
**Goal:** Use AI to eliminate administrative friction and mitigate counterparty risk.

*   **Modules:** AI Aluminium Assistant, Company Intelligence.
*   **Features:**
    *   Conversational AI for analyzing complex trade agreements and L/C terms.
    *   Automated KYC, sanctions checking, and credit risk profiling.
    *   Fraud detection for warehouse warrants and invoices.
    *   Technical translation between global alloy standards (AA, EN, GB).

---

## Phase 4 — Market Intelligence
**Goal:** Provide a centralized "Glass House" for pricing, volatility, and profit optimization.

*   **Modules:** Market Intelligence.
*   **Features:**
    *   Live LME Ticker (Cash/3-Month) and spread analysis.
    *   Physical premium index tracking (Rotterdam, Midwest, MJP).
    *   Scrap-to-Primary spread analytics for recycling margin optimization.
    *   Dynamic hedging recommendation engine.

---

## Phase 5 — Enterprise Platform
**Goal:** Scale the system for global trade houses with complex organizational needs.

*   **Modules:** Finance Integration, Team Collaboration, Global Logistics.
*   **Features:**
    *   Multi-currency accounting and VAT handling for cross-border trade.
    *   Role-based access control (RBAC) for large trade desks.
    *   Direct API integrations with shipping lines and major banks.
    *   Advanced analytics for team performance and regional market share.
